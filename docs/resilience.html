Resilience

failure injection · adequacy statistics

Inject equipment trips, storms and demand surges into one configuration and compare the stressed trajectory against an identical-weather baseline — or sweep randomly generated fleets through batches of failure scenarios to see which component mixes hold up. Metrics follow standard adequacy definitions: loss-of-load probability (LOLP), loss-of-load expectation (LOLE) and energy not served (ENS).

System under test

Baseline and stressed runs share the same weather draw (same seed), so every delta in the results is attributable to the injected failures alone.
